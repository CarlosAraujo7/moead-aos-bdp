## Third-party material (not covered by the licences above)

* **Benchmark instances** are not redistributed in this repository. MDPLIB 2.0 (Martí, Duarte, Martínez-Gavara and Sánchez-Oro) is distributed by its authors; the CDP benchmark is the one of Peiró et al. (2021); the GDP benchmark and its solutions are the Zenodo record 18233882 of Lozano-Osorio et al. (2022), released under CC BY 4.0. Each user must obtain them from the original sources and respect their terms.
* **Values transcribed from the literature** in `anchors/` (best published values of the capacitated dispersion problem: Gomez et al. 2026, Lu et al. 2023, Rosati and Schaerf 2024, Mladenović et al. 2022) belong to the cited works and are included only for comparison, with the citation.
* `instances/MDPLIB_2_0.pdf` is the documentation of MDPLIB 2.0 and remains the property of its authors.
